About this project

Why StockSense exists

Most small and mid-sized operations still run inventory on paper registers and disconnected spreadsheets — no single source of truth, no live view of what's on a shelf, and no record of who moved what and when. StockSense was built to replace that with one modular, real-time system that any inventory manager or warehouse worker can pick up in minutes.

The objective is simple: every movement of stock — in from a supplier, out to a customer, across warehouses, or corrected after a physical count — passes through the same ledger. That gives a business a live dashboard of what's low, what's pending, and where every unit physically sits, without a single manual register.
